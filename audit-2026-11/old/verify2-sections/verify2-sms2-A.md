### SMS-005 — VERIFIED-DRIFT (byte totals overstated; divergence exact)

### SMS-012 — VERIFIED-DRIFT (first copy is now a named helper; duplication itself intact)

### SMS-013 — REFUTED (the video ring is already allocated only when there is a video stream)
The finding's central claim — "An MP3 or AAC stream allocates the same 2 MB video ring as a video file… both allocated for every playback (SMS_Player.c:1741-1777)" — is defeated by the allocation sites' own guards, re-read in full:

- `src/SMS_Player.c:54-55` defines `SMS_VP_BUFFER_SIZE (1024*1024*2)` and `SMS_AP_BUFFER_SIZE (1024*512)` — the sizes are as claimed.
- The video allocation at :1741 (`s_pVPBuff = SMS_SyncMalloc(SMS_VP_BUFFER_SIZE)`) sits inside the per-stream loop under `if (lpStm->m_pCodec->m_Type == SMS_CodecTypeVideo)` (:1716), further nested under `if (s_Player.m_VideoIdx < 0)` and a successful `SMS_CodecOpen` / codec `Init`. An audio-only container has no video stream, so this code never executes for it.
- The audio allocation at :1776-1777 sits in the separate `else if (… m_Type == SMS_CodecTypeAudio)` branch (:1759) and is itself guarded by `if (!lpAudioBuffer)`.

So the proposed fix ("allocate the video ring only when the container has a video stream") is **already the code at the pin**, and the 2 MB saving on audio-only playback does not exist to be taken. What remains true is only the weaker, unremarkable fact that each ring, *when its stream type is present*, is a fixed size (2 MB video / 512 KB audio) not scaled to bitrate — the finding's secondary audio-sizing suggestion, which was labelled INFERRED in the original. That residue does not support the finding as written.

### SMS-014 (as revised, R2-1) — VERIFIED-DRIFT (revision's substance holds; its per-type call counts do not reproduce)

- **M3U sites not overflows:** `lBuf[512]`/`lTitle[512]` at `SMS_ContainerM3U.c:289-290`; :351 `strcpy(lTitle, lpPtr+1)` copies a substring of the capped `lBuf`; :371 copies into `malloc(strlen(lBuf)+1)`; :390 copies a substring of an exact allocation. Confirmed.

- **Population recount:** `grep -rn -E '\b(strcpy|strcat|sprintf)\s*\(' src --include='*.c'` = **238 distinct call lines**, exactly the revised population, and 7+48+183 = 238 is arithmetically consistent. Spot-checked members of each surviving class: CDDA bypass (A) at `SMS_FileDir.c:465-466` vs the capacity-checked `_append_directory_path` at :242-261; FOURCC B-reads at `SMS_GUInfoPanel.c:110,118,121` (`sprintf(…, (char*)&lpCodec->m_Tag, …)`).

### SMS-034 — WEAKENED (vendoring proven; "unused" never proven)
What is proven at the pin: `fonts/` holds 19 tracked files in three vendor-named trees with spaces in paths (`CyrFonts_by_Deank/` 12 `.mtf` in 6 subfolders, `Hebrew/` 3, `Turkish by nethunter/` 4), and fonts are loaded by explicit bare filename, not by scanning (`src/SMS_Config.c:56-60`: `ascii.mtf`, `latin2.mtf`, `cyrillic.mtf`, `latin1.mtf`, `greek.mtf`). What is **not** proven — the finding itself flags it INFERRED and admits "a per-file grep of configs was not done" — is that specific pack members are unreferenced: the packs are alternative per-language sets sharing bare names (six different `ascii.mtf`/`cyrillic.mtf` pairs, one per typeface), so duplication-by-name across subfolders is the pack structure, not evidence of dead files; and two names the code *does* reference (`greek.mtf`, `latin2.mtf`) appear in **no** vendored pack, which cuts against a simple "keep only referenced files" sweep. Exact narrower truth: whole third-party font packs with space-laden vendor paths are vendored, SMS loads fonts by bare name from the memory card (not from this tree), and whether any given `.mtf` here is ever copied/used is not determinable from the repo.

### SMS-017 / SMS-027 / SMS-028 revised-claim notes (all VERIFIED, recorded here for the ledger)
