## AAC rate finding in #46

The AVI AAC tag/configuration mapping is supported, but enabling it also
offers the existing 22,050 Hz setting. A synthesized FFmpeg output has WAVE
rate 22,050 and AAC-LC AudioSpecificConfig `139056e500`. Source inspection
shows that AAC initialization implicitly upsamples this configuration, while
`_aac_init` previously left the context at the container's rate.

`_aac_decode` emits 2,048 PCM frames per access unit in this mode. The player
copies `m_SampleRate` after initialization, so retaining 22,050 would represent
twice the intended duration. The expected relationship is:

| Encoded rate | PCM frames per access unit | Playback rate | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 22,050 Hz | 2,048 | 44,100 Hz | 1,024 / 22,050 seconds |
| 48,000 Hz | 1,024 | 48,000 Hz | 1,024 / 48,000 seconds |

On successful initialization, publish the effective PCM rate from the decoder's
frequency index and exactly the same doubling condition used by decoding:
SBR or forced upsampling, without downsampled SBR. Do not simply copy the
initializer's returned rate: configuration parsing can double that value even
when implicit upsampling is disabled. Leave the separate stream rate used for
encoded packet timestamps unchanged.

The change is idempotent across seek/reinitialization and applies before player
startup, track switching and playlist rate refresh. Failed initialization keeps
the previous context rate. The decoder's spectral reconstruction and channel
policy are unchanged.
